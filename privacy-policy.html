Privacy Policy

Arrows - Advanced Puzzle · Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary

Arrows - Advanced Puzzle ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and you do not need an account to play. The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers optional in-app purchases (Google Play). These services may process some device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, hints), settings (sound, music, vibration, Safe Tap) and purchases you made (for example "Remove Ads") are saved only on your device so the game works offline. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about ad views and interactions, and diagnostics.

This is used to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. We never receive or store your payment card or bank details. Google shares only the purchase result (which item was bought) with the game so it can unlock what you paid for. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Unity Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent at any time in Settings > Privacy options (where available).
  • Turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the game.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us and we will help, or contact Google directly for data it processes.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google AdMob, Google Play, Unity) so the game can show ads and process purchases. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
